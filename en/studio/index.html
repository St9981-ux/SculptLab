From one place to another

The imagined.
The hand.
The trace.

Some journeys leave no address behind. We bring back a face, the curve of a horn, the feeling of having been watched. The sculptures begin there: in what persists when everything else fades.

“I have wandered through lands that time has forgotten, realms erased from human memory, and others untouched by any name.”

From memory to matter

Give a body
to what remains.

01 / Shape

Recover a contour.

The original model is shaped by hand. One volume calls for another, until a silhouette seems to recognise its own form.

02 / Cast

Let the echo endure.

The model is cast in resin. Open editions, limited series and one-of-a-kind pieces allow the encounter to continue in different forms.

03 / Paint

Change the light.

Spray, airbrush, brush. Each sculpture is painted by hand. A colour appears, and already the memory is not quite the same.

Io, Za’mu, Enigma

Worlds
to welcome.

Io, Za’mu and Enigma do not reveal everything. They keep something of the place they came from. Sometimes, living beside a work is enough to hear what it had left unspoken.
