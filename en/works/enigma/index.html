The story of Enigma

That presence…

That presence… Its memory chills me still, even today. He stood there, imposing, his curved horns cutting through the night like a crescent moon. His single eye pierced me, probing the most secret folds of my being, as though he were their master. All around me, everyone bowed, trembling, and I too could not resist. It was neither faith nor merely fear, but the certainty of standing before something infinitely beyond me.
